1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from AFC Urgent Care Willowbrook, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name

- Communication preferences
- Message interaction data such as delivery confirmations

2. How We Use SMS Messaging

AFC Urgent Care Willowbrook uses SMS messaging to communicate with patients regarding:
- Appointment scheduling and confirmations
- Appointment reminders
- Visit updates
- Follow-up communication related to care
- General customer service communications

SMS messages are conversational in nature and are intended to support patient care and clinic operations.

3. SMS Consent

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from AFC Urgent Care Willowbrook. Consent is not a condition of receiving medical treatment or purchasing services.

Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with the clinic.

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

4. Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:
STOP

After you send STOP, you may receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly at +1 (331) 251-4805.

5. Information Sharing

AFC Urgent Care Willowbrook does not sell, rent, or share your mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with service providers that assist us in delivering SMS communications, provided they are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. HIPAA and Healthcare Communications

As a healthcare provider, AFC Urgent Care Willowbrook may communicate certain health-related information via SMS. Patients should be aware that text messaging may not always be fully secure. Sensitive or urgent medical matters should be discussed directly with a healthcare professional by phone or in person.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.
